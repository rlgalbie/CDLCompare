## 2) Executive Summary

- The market splits into two models:
  - **Immediate CDL model** (Roehl, Knight, Swift, Schneider, possibly Eagle) with faster licensing but higher lock-in risk.
  - **Dock-to-driver model** (ODFL, XPO, Saia) with slower progression but typically better first-job quality and flexibility.
- Most immediate-CDL programs lead to OTR first-seat jobs, usually with less home time at start.
- LTL dock-to-driver paths often improve local/regional outcomes but are less predictable on timeline.
- The most decision-critical unknowns are still contract details, repayment triggers, and Phoenix-specific class/start timing.
- Use written terms (program docs, offer letters, recruiter email confirmation) as final authority over verbal claims.

## 3) How to Read Confidence

- **Confirmed**: directly supported by official company page or equivalent primary source.
- **Likely**: supported by partial primary data and/or consistent secondary context, but not fully explicit.
- **Unverified**: unclear, conflicting, or not found in accessible source material.

## 4) Phoenix/Chandler Market Structure

### Immediate CDL Track (fast path)

Common pattern:

- Time to CDL: usually about 3-8 weeks depending on carrier/program phase.
- Entry: no CDL required at application.
- Financial model: contract, reimbursement, or payroll deduction/forgiveness.
- First-seat role: usually OTR or extended regional.

### Dock-to-Driver Track (delayed path)

Common pattern:

- Time to CDL: often tied to internal promotion windows (can be months).
- Entry: dock/warehouse role first.
- Financial model: generally lower direct training repayment risk.
- First-seat role: often local/regional after promotion.

## 7) Eligibility and Disqualifier Checklist (What to Verify Before Applying)

Use this checklist per company:

- Minimum age requirements for interstate work
- MVR standards and violation lookback
- DUI and serious infraction policies
- Drug testing method (urine vs hair vs both)
- DOT medical card readiness
- Criminal background lookback rules
- Rehire and prior-employer verification policies
- Automatic vs manual training/testing and any license restriction impact

---

## 8) Financial Risk Checklist (Most Important Before Signing)

Collect these in writing before committing:

- Full tuition/training cost amount
- Whether cost is waived, reimbursed, deducted, or forgiven over time
- Repayment trigger events (quit, termination, fail out, safety event)
- Liability schedule at month 1, 3, 6, and 12
- How repayment is collected and under what deadlines

## 10) Practical Decision Framework

If your priority is **speed to CDL**:

- Focus on Roehl, Knight, Swift, Schneider.
- Accept likely OTR first-seat placement and verify contract exposure carefully.

If your priority is **first-job quality and local lifestyle**:

- Focus on ODFL, XPO, Saia dock-to-driver routes.
- Accept slower timeline and verify promotion criteria before committing.

If your priority is **lowest financial lock-in risk**:

- Prioritize programs with explicit, written no-contract or low-penalty terms.
- Avoid any offer where repayment language is vague or verbal-only.

## 11) Standard Verification Script (Use for All 8)

Ask each recruiter these exact questions and request written confirmation:

1. What is the exact CDL path from day 1 to solo dispatch?
2. What pay do I receive during school and during trainer/finishing weeks?
3. What is the exact tuition/repayment structure and trigger language?
4. What amount would I owe if I leave at 3, 6, and 12 months?
5. For Phoenix hires, where is orientation and where is first dispatch?
6. What first-seat type should I expect (OTR/regional/local), with expected home time?
7. Is training/test automatic or manual, and will I have restrictions?
8. Is there a current class waitlist for Phoenix candidates?
